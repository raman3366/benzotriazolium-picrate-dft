Table: Proton position in the benzotriazolium picrate pair by level of theory, all with the 6-311++G(d,p) basis set at B3LYP/6-311++G(d,p) geometries. Gas phase: relaxed B3LYP scan of *r*(H4N···O1) from 1.70 to 0.90 Å (16 points); the other levels are single points at the scan geometries. Δ*E*(ionic − neutral) is the energy of the published ionic minimum (*r*(H4N···O1) = 1.437 Å) relative to the lowest point of the profile (0.98 Å, proton on O1), which is the same point at every level; positive values mean that the neutral form is lower. The fully optimised B3LYP neutral minimum lies 2.16 kcal mol⁻¹ below the ionic one. The ionic well is the rise from the lowest point with the proton on N4 to the highest point on the way to O1; "none" means that the energy falls all the way from the ionic geometry to the neutral one. ZPE is the ground level of the proton (one-dimensional sinc-DVR along its path on the scan, mass of H) above the potential minimum (329–502 cm⁻¹), and *P*(O side) is its probability with *r*(N4–H4N) > *r*(H4N···O1). IEF-PCM (water): energy of the neutral pair (proton on O1) minus that of the salt, both at their B3LYP IEF-PCM minima; positive values mean that the salt is lower.
